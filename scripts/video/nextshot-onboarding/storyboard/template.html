How to review

  1. Watch the whole video once with sound. Then watch it once muted, as most people see it in Teams and Outlook.
  2. Go card by card. Mark each line Keep, Change, or Cut. To change the words, type the new line in "Rewrite the line".
  3. Add notes on the picture and the timing. When you finish, tell Claude that the notes are in.

Words an editor uses

Voiceover
the voice you hear but never see. Here it is {{VOICE}}.
Caption
the spoken words on screen. Most people watch muted.
Scene
one part of the story with one job. This cut has {{SCENE_COUNT}}.
Beat
one moment inside a scene, such as a badge that pops in.
Cue
a planned moment when a picture change or a sound hits a word.
Sound effect
a short sound for an action: a pop, a click, a camera shutter.
Music bed
the quiet music under the whole video. It sets the pace and mood.
Ducking
the music gets quieter while the voice talks, then comes back up.
Lead-in
the silence at the start of a scene, before its first line.
Tail
the silence after a scene's last line, before the next scene starts.
Hold
time the picture stays still, so the viewer can read it.
Cut and wipe
a cut changes the picture at once. A wipe reveals the new picture with a moving shape, such as the teal circle at "Meet Nextshot."
Timecode
a time in the video, as minutes:seconds. 0:12.1 is 12.1 seconds in.

Rules of thumb

  • The hook must land in the first 3 seconds (Zubtitle).
  • The video must work muted, so every line has a caption (same source).
  • Explainer narration runs about 150 words per minute, or 2.5 words per second (Breadnbeyond). Short, punchy lines can run faster. Each card shows its speed.
