## Send from the website

The [Nextshot feedback form](https://feedback.nextshot.dev/) is available now. Choose a category, describe what happened, and select **Send feedback**. Add an email address if you want a reply.

You can attach PNG and JPEG screenshots. Attachments are optional. The form accepts up to 10 MB total.

Review every screenshot and note before sending. Remove private information first.

Files stay private. Screening checks for malware, sensitive information, and suspicious instructions. A completed scan does not guarantee safety. Read [Privacy and security](https://feedback.nextshot.dev/privacy.html) for details.

## Send a round from Nextshot

Open a round's submenu in **Recent Rounds**. Choose **Submit Round to Nextshot…**. Add a note and an optional reply email, then review the panel before you choose **Submit**.

Nextshot checks the package locally before sending it. Native submissions send annotated screenshots and notes. Clean copies and stored screen text stay on your Mac.

Visible screenshots and notes can contain private information. Review them before submitting. Read the receipt for the submission result.
