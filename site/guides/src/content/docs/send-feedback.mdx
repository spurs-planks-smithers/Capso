## Send from the website

The [Nextshot feedback form](https://feedback.nextshot.dev/) is available now. Choose a category, describe what happened, and select **Send feedback**. Add an email address if you want a reply.

You can attach a Nextshot zip or PNG and JPEG screenshots. Attachments are optional. The form accepts one zip per submission and up to 10 MB total.

Review every screenshot and note before sending. Standard Nextshot exports include clean screenshots, screen text, window titles, and notes. Remove private information first.

Files stay private. Screening checks for malware, sensitive information, and suspicious instructions. A completed scan does not guarantee safety. Read [Privacy and security](https://feedback.nextshot.dev/privacy.html) for details.

## Native submission is coming in 0.5.0

Nextshot 0.4.0 does not show a native submission action. Use the website form now.

Version 0.5.0 will add **Submit Round to Nextshot…** to each round's submenu. The panel will let you add a note and optional reply email before you press **Submit**.

Native submissions will send annotated screenshots and notes after local screening. Clean copies and stored screen text will stay on your Mac.
