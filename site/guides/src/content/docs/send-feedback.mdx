## Send from the website

The [Nextshot feedback form](https://feedback.nextshot.dev/) is available now. Choose a category, describe what happened, and select **Send feedback**. Add an email address if you want a reply.

You can attach PNG and JPEG screenshots. Attachments are optional. The form accepts up to 10 MB total.

**The form is not on-device. Read this before you attach anything.**

Everything else in Nextshot stays on your Mac. The feedback form does not. A screenshot you attach leaves your Mac and reaches a server, where it is stored and screened. Nothing you attach can be taken back once you send it.

**A screenshot is a full picture of your screen.** Marks are pointers, not redaction: a red dot sits on top of the thing it marks, and what is under it is still in the picture. Text you circled, a name, an email address, a customer's order, a key, a web address with a token in it — all of it arrives with the file.

**Remove private information before you attach anything.** Cover or crop it in another app first. If you are unsure, leave the screenshot out. Describe what happened in words: a description usually carries the problem and none of the risk.

Files stay private once we have them. Screening checks for malware, sensitive information, and suspicious instructions, and the server can hold or reject a submission that fails a check. A completed scan does not guarantee safety, and it does not make a private detail private again. Read [Privacy and security](https://feedback.nextshot.dev/privacy.html) for details.

## Send a round from Nextshot

Open a round's submenu in **Recent Rounds**. Choose **Submit Round to Nextshot…**. Add a note and an optional reply email, then review the panel before you choose **Submit**.

Nextshot checks the package locally before sending it. Native submissions send annotated screenshots and notes. Clean copies and stored screen text stay on your Mac.

**A round also leaves your Mac when you submit it,** under the same rules as the form above: annotated screenshots and notes are sent, and screening happens after that. Review the panel before you choose **Submit**, and leave out a capture that shows something private. Read the receipt for the submission result.
