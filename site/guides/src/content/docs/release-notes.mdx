## 0.6.0

November 1, 2026 (UTC)

- Press **Option+Shift+G** to capture the same screen area as your last capture, with no dragging.
- Press **Esc** on a marked capture to copy the marked image and save it to the round with no notes. The round stays open.
- Copied prompts start with `Session Name:`, the app you captured, and a name for the round. On a Mac with macOS 26 or newer and Apple Intelligence on, Apple's on-device model writes the name as a summary of your captures. Nothing leaves your Mac.
- Recent Rounds labels each round with your first instruction.
- About 7 days after you first run a build, Nextshot shows one reminder to check nextshot.dev for a newer version. Turn it off in **Settings > General**.

## 0.5.0

October 28, 2026 (UTC)

- Submit a round to Nextshot from its menu. Review the annotated screenshots and notes before sending.
- Find older rounds through **Show More**, grouped by day, week, and month. Compact app names keep capture counts visible.
- Save your initial startup choice with **Done**. Updates preserve your choice to keep startup off.
- Get screen access help beside **Screen Recording**. Settings use clearer pointer labels and shortcut groups.
- Clear assigned shortcut keys with the x control. The action stays available in the menu.
- Open the website feedback form from **Report a Bug** or **Request a Feature**. New Settings guides explain the available controls.

## 0.4.0

October 27, 2026 (UTC)

- Follow visual Guides for your first handoff. Search the instructions or download the printable shortcut sheet.
- Settings focus on feedback rounds, with fewer controls for unrelated tools.
- Bundle manifests list annotated images for handoff to your AI agent.

## 0.3.0

October 26, 2026 (UTC)

- Start marking with the red dot tool. The first click adds dot 1.
- Find the active round first and the newest captures first in **Recent Rounds**.
- **End Round** copies the round prompt, just like **Copy Prompt and End Round**.
- Install updates directly from the disk image. The installer replaces a running copy and opens the new version.
- Read the help box at the bottom right. Confirmations stay visible longer.

[Install the latest version](/guides/install/#install-an-update).
