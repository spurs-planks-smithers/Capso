## Open Recent Rounds

Click the Nshot menu. **Recent Rounds** lists the active round, once it has a saved capture, followed by the five most recent ended rounds.

Each round shows a name of five words or fewer. On a Mac with macOS 26 or newer and Apple Intelligence on, the name is a short summary of your notes. Apple's on-device model writes the summary, and nothing leaves your Mac. On other Macs, the name is your first instruction, cut to five words. A round with no written instruction shows its time as the name. The same round can show a different name on another Mac.

The prompt you copy from a round starts with `Session Name:`, the app your screenshots show, and the same name. Your agent can then list each round under its own name. The line below the name shows the time, the apps it includes, and its capture count. Open the round’s submenu for its actions and captures.

Choose **Show More** for older ended rounds. Open a date, week, or month group to find its rounds. Their submenus offer the same copy, folder, and resume actions.

Long app lists use compact names and a +N count. Capture and selection counts stay visible.

The newest capture appears first in the menu. The files and the agent’s item numbers keep their original capture order.

Each capture row shows its time and a short name for what you marked and wrote. A capture with no summary shows its page or window name instead.

## Find a capture

Choose **Find Capture** in the Nshot menu. A window lists every saved capture, newest first, grouped by day. The captures of the active round are in the list too. Nextshot 0.8.0 and later has Find Capture.

Type in the search field at the top. Nextshot looks in the app name, the window title, your instructions and notes, the round name, and the capture name. Each word you type must appear. Capital letters and accents do not matter. Text that Nextshot read from the screen is not searched. To see the captures of one app, choose it in the app menu next to the search field.

Click a capture to check it. These are the same checks as in the Nshot menu. Choose **Copy N Selected** or **Zip N Selected** at the bottom of the window to use them. Choose **Clear** to remove the checks. After a click, press Space to see the capture in Quick Look. Type a letter to go back to the search.

Click the round label on a capture to see only that round. Your search still applies. The bar at the bottom then shows **Copy Round Prompt**, **Zip Round**, **Open Folder**, and **Resume Round**. **Resume Round** shows only when no round is active. Choose **Back** to see every round again.

Press Esc to close the window.

## Copy a round again

Choose **Copy Round Prompt** in the round’s submenu. Paste the prompt into the agent that works on your project.

This copy does not end an active round. It also tells the agent to check the code before repeating earlier work.

## Copy a selection

1. Open a round’s submenu.
2. Click the captures you want. Each selected row gets a check.
3. Select captures from another round if needed.
4. Choose **Copy N Selected**. It appears at the top of every round’s submenu and in the main Nshot menu.

Nextshot copies one combined prompt for every checked capture, including captures in other rounds, and clears the checks. One checked capture gives you that capture’s prompt.

The rows stay gray until you check a capture, so the list never moves while you click. Choose **Clear Selection** to remove the checks without copying.

## Send a round as a zip

Use a zip to give a round to someone on another Mac, or to send it in mail, Slack, or Teams.

1. Open a round’s submenu.
2. Choose **Zip Round**. It sits below **Copy Round Prompt**.

To zip only some captures, check their rows, then choose **Zip N Selected**. It appears at the top of every round’s submenu and in the main Nshot menu.

Nextshot saves the zip in your Downloads folder and copies the file. A message shows its name and size, and the macOS Share menu opens at the Nshot icon. Paste the file into a message, or choose an app in the Share menu. The first zip asks for access to Downloads. Choose Allow.

The zip holds a `README.md` with the whole prompt, plus the images and notes of each capture. A zip does not end a round. **Zip N Selected** clears the checks, as **Copy N Selected** does. Nextshot 0.8.0 and later has Zip.

## Preview an image

Rest the pointer on a capture row for a second. A large preview of the annotated image appears beside the menu. It disappears when you move off the row.

You can also hold Option and click a capture row. Nextshot opens its annotated image in Quick Look. This does not select the capture.

## Resume a round

End the current round first, if one is active. Open the earlier round’s submenu, then choose **Resume Round**.

Your next saved capture joins that round. Its numbering continues from the existing captures.

Resume appears only when no other round is active.

## Open the files

Choose **Open Folder** in the round’s submenu. Each round has its own folder with the screenshots, notes, and prompts.

If you are looking for older work, the Nshot menu also provides **All Rounds**. Recent Rounds keeps its list short for quick access.
