These screenshots use Nextshot Dev. The controls are the same in Nextshot.

<Step number={1} title="Open Settings">

</Step>

## Where rounds stay

Round files stay in `~/Library/Application Support/Nextshot`. Open the current folder with **Open Round Folder** in the Nshot menu.

Nextshot and Nextshot Dev share this folder. They keep separate settings and separate screen access grants.

[Connect your agent](/guides/connect-agent/) explains how to give it access to the round files.
