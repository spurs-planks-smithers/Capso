Keyboard controls

Return saves your note and advances. Shift+Return adds a new line. Esc focuses the dot tabs. A number selects its dot. G selects General note. Left/Right changes dots; Home selects the first. Tab moves to the note.

Tab and Shift+Tab move between controls. Enter activates the focused button. N, A, and R choose dot, arrow, and box tools outside typing. Command/Ctrl+Z undoes marks outside typing. Command/Ctrl+W keeps Chrome’s close-tab behavior.

After saving: C copies instructions, X downloads the ZIP, and Command/Ctrl+W closes the tab. Shift+X discards an unsaved capture outside typing.
